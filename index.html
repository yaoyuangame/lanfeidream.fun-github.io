About Us

Who we are

LanFei Dream was founded with a simple belief: games should be easy to pick up, hard to put down, and fun for everyone. We are a team of developers, artists, and dreamers who love making games that fit into daily life.

Our games are designed for short sessions, instant enjoyment, and universal appeal. We focus on clean mechanics, charming presentation, and experiences that respect the player's time.

Our Mission To create simple, delightful games that bring joy to players around the world.
Our Approach Small teams, focused scope, polished execution. We do a few things and do them well.
Our Audience Casual players of all ages who want a quick, relaxing escape.

What We Believe

Our values

Accessible by Design
Anyone should be able to play our games. Simple rules, intuitive controls, no barriers.
Quality over Quantity
We would rather ship one polished game than many forgettable ones.
Player First
Every decision starts with the question: does this make the game more fun?
Respect Your Time
Our games respect your schedule. Jump in for two minutes or two hours.
